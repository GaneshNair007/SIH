# OISD-STD-155 (Parts I & II): Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Guidelines

## Section 3.4: Respiratory Protection in Sour Gas Environments
- **Respirator Selection:** In areas with potential H2S concentrations up to 10 ppm, half-mask or full-face negative pressure air-purifying respirators equipped with chemical cartridges specifically rated for Acid Gases / H2S must be utilized.
- **Cartridge Service Life:** Cartridges must be replaced:
  1. Immediately following any shift where cumulative exposure exceeded Tier 2 threshold (TWA >= 1.0 ppm or shift dose >= 8.0 ppm·hr).
  2. If breathing resistance increases or odor breakthrough is detected.
  3. Every 30 days regardless of exposure under standard operating rotations.
- **Fit Testing:** Quantitative or qualitative negative/positive pressure user seal checks must be performed before every entry. Annual quantitative fit-testing documentation is mandatory.
- **Decontamination & First Aid:**
  - Emergency eye wash stations must be installed within 10 seconds (approx. 15 meters) of hazardous sour points.
  - Workers experiencing conjunctival irritation must perform a continuous 15-minute eye flush with isotonic sterile saline or running potable water.
