# ACGIH & NIOSH Hydrogen Sulfide (H2S) Exposure Thresholds

## Exposure Thresholds & Biological Responses
- **ACGIH TLV-TWA:** 1.0 ppm (8-hour time-weighted average).
- **ACGIH TLV-STEL:** 5.0 ppm (15-minute short-term exposure limit).
- **NIOSH REL:** 10.0 ppm Ceiling (10-minute limit).
- **NIOSH IDLH:** 100.0 ppm (Immediately Dangerous to Life or Health).

## Dose-Response Symptoms & Olfactory Fatigue:
- **0.01 - 0.3 ppm:** Odor threshold (pungent rotten egg smell).
- **1.0 - 5.0 ppm:** Mild conjunctival and upper respiratory tract irritation. Noticeable odor.
- **5.0 - 10.0 ppm:** Olfactory fatigue / nerve paralysis onset. Workers may falsely perceive that gas has cleared.
- **20.0 - 50.0 ppm:** Severe ocular tearing, photophobia, intense headache, marked bronchial irritation.
- **100.0+ ppm:** Rapid olfactory paralysis, pulmonary edema risk, loss of consciousness ("knockdown").

## Self-Care & Hydration Protocols:
- Fresh air rest in positive-pressure control building for minimum 20 minutes.
- Copious hydration and skin wash with clean water to prevent dermal sulfur residue adsorption.
- Strictly avoid alcohol or CNS depressants within 24 hours following toxic gas exposure.
