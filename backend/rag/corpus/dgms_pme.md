# DGMS Circulars & Periodical Medical Examination (PME) Standards

## Section 2: Occupational Health Centre (OHC) Referral Standards
- **Mandatory Clinical Referral:**
  - Any worker with shift TWA >= 5.0 ppm or rolling 7-day load >= 35.0 ppm·hr must be sent for clinical evaluation at the refinery Occupational Health Centre (OHC).
  - Clinical battery must include:
    1. Arterial Blood Oxygen Saturation (SpO2) monitoring.
    2. Peak Expiratory Flow Rate (PEFR) / Bedside Spirometry compared to baseline FEV1/FVC.
    3. Slit-lamp ocular examination for keratoconjunctivitis (gas eye).
- **Work Restriction:** The worker must be restricted from entering hazardous sour gas areas for a minimum of 48 hours or until cleared by the Factory Medical Officer.
- **Medical Records:** All dosimeter readings and corresponding OHC triage sheets must be maintained in the worker's permanent occupational health register for a statutory period of 30 years.
