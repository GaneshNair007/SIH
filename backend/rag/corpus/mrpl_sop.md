# MRPL Mangalore Refinery Standard Operating Procedures: Sour Processing Units

## Unit Specific Safety Matrices:
1. **CDU-1 / CDU-2 (Crude Distillation Units):** Overhead condenser boots, desalter water drains, and sour water strippers represent high-risk H2S release nodes.
2. **DHDS (Diesel Hydrodesulfurization):** High-pressure hydrogen recycle loop, amine absorption towers, and stripper overheads contain concentrated H2S (> 5000 ppm stream concentration). Full-face respirators and dosimeter bands required for all sampling.
3. **SRU (Sulfur Recovery Unit / Claus Plant):** Reaction furnace, sulfur pit vent headers, and tail gas treatment units (TGTU). Mandatory buddy system.
4. **Tank Farm & Flare Headers:** Floating roof sour crude storage tanks, sour water storage tank roofs, and liquid knock-out drums.

## Shift Supervisor Shift-End Checklist:
- Reconcile shift dosimeter scans of all unit technicians.
- In case of any Tier 2 scan, flag plant unit tag for seal and flange sniffing on the subsequent shift.
- In case of Tier 3 scan, issue Form-A immediately, inform HSE Department Head, and ensure immediate worker transfer to OHC.
