# OISD-STD-105: Work Permit System & Hazardous Gas Precautions

## Section 4.2: Hydrogen Sulfide (H2S) Hazardous Area Precautions
- Any hydrocarbon processing unit handling sour crude or sour gas streams (e.g. Crude Distillation Unit, Diesel Hydrodesulfurization, Sulfur Recovery Unit) must establish strict entry control and dosimeter tracking.
- **Continuous Monitoring:** Workers entering designated Class-I H2S zones must wear calibrated passive dosimeter badges or personal gas detectors.
- **Exposure Actions:**
  - If personal TWA exceeds 1.0 ppm, work permits for subsequent shifts must be reviewed and respiratory protection verified.
  - If personal TWA reaches or exceeds 5.0 ppm, hot work permits in the respective zone are immediately suspended. Shift supervisor must initiate immediate line walk, valve sniffing, and isolation of fugitive flange leaks.
- **Emergency Reporting:** Any acute exposure incident resulting in TWA >= 5.0 ppm or single-shift dose > 20.0 ppm·hr requires mandatory filing of OISD Incident Form-A within 24 hours to the refinery Safety Department.
