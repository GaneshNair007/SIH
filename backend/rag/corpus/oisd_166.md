# OISD-STD-166 / 114: Hazardous Area Classification & Flange Inspection

## Section 5.1: Fugitive Emission Containment in Sour Processing
- **Zone Tagging:** Process areas with sulfur-containing hydrocarbon streams are classified under Class-I Division 1/2.
- **Fugitive Leak Investigation:** Whenever personal dosimeters of operating personnel indicate Tier 2 or Tier 3 exposures, the shift in-charge must deploy portable toxic vapor analyzers (PID/FID) to sniff:
  1. Pump and compressor mechanical seals.
  2. Valve packing glands and blind flanges in the suspected sub-zone.
  3. Closed sample point drains and flare header condensate traps.
- **Ventilation Protocols:** Positive-pressure shelter rooms must maintain minimum 0.1 inch water gauge positive static pressure to prevent sour gas ingress. Workers must rest in positive-pressure shelters post-shift.
